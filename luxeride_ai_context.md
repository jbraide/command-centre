## 🗣️ 5. Tone, Voice & Language Guidelines

- **Tone:** Conversational, authentic, practical, slightly urgent but never pushy
- **Language:** Nigerian English, relatable idioms (`kakaraka`, `thank me later`, `no wahala`)
- **Avoid:** Corporate jargon, exaggerated claims, fake urgency, "luxury-only" framing
- **Rule:** Sound like a trusted mechanic or experienced driver, not a salesperson
- **Silent-Optimized:** 70% watch muted. Every key benefit must appear as on-screen text.

## 🎬 6. Proven Script Structure (The "Show, Tell, Invite" Formula)

```
[0:00–0:04] HOOK: Show problem → solution instantly
[0:04–0:12] DEMO: Visual proof of product feature (water test, fit, thickness)
[0:12–0:20] DIFFERENTIATE: Compare single vs double, or cheap vs LuxeRide
[0:20–0:26] REASSURE: "Fits any model", "Live photos before dispatch", "Pay on delivery"
[0:26–0:30] CTA: Low-friction → "DM your car model + year. We confirm in 2 mins."
```

> 💡 **AI Rule:** Max 30 seconds. 150 words max. Cut every 1.5–2s. Match visuals to VO beats.

## 🤖 12. AI Script Generation Rules & Constraints

1. **Never invent prices.** Use `"From ₦48,000"` or `"DM for exact quote"`.
2. **Always match product to buyer type.** Single Layer = practical/durability. Double Layer = premium/strength.
3. **Max 30 seconds / 150 words.** Cut fluff. Every line must serve hook, proof, or CTA.
4. **Include on-screen text for every VO claim.** (Silent optimization)
5. **Use Nigerian context naturally.** Mention Lagos rain, harmattan dust, daily traffic, salary weeks.
6. **End with low-friction CTA.** `"DM your car model + year. We confirm fit & price in 2 mins."`
7. **Provide 3 hook variations** per script (Value, Problem-Solution, Social Proof).
8. **Output format:** Table with Time | Visual | Voiceover | On-Screen Text | Audio Cues.
